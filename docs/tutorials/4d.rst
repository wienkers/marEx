=====================
Ocean Extremes in 4-D
=====================

Marine heatwaves at depth, tracked as volumes. Thirty years of daily potential temperature
from the 1/12° GLORYS12 reanalysis, on 36 levels from the surface to 1150 m over the
northeast Pacific, go through the same :func:`marEx.preprocess_data` call as the surface
examples, with a fixed 1993-2022 baseline and the day-of-year threshold. See
:ref:`extra-dimensions` for the rules. The tracker then follows each event through
``(time, depth, lat, lon)`` as a volume (see :ref:`tracking-in-4d`), and the last notebook
draws the events of the 2013-16 Blob as isosurfaces, sections and movies.

The first notebook downloads the input and runs in its own environment.
