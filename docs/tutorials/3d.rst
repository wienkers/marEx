=====================
Ocean Extremes in 3-D
=====================

Detection on a field with a depth axis. A century of daily potential temperature from
the EERIE IFS-FESOM2-SR control run, on seven levels from the surface to 1415 m over the
North Atlantic, goes through the same :func:`marEx.preprocess_data` call as the surface
examples, with a shifting baseline and the day-of-year threshold. The notebook then looks
at extremes level by level, in depth-time sections and as vertical profiles. See
:ref:`extra-dimensions` for the rules.
