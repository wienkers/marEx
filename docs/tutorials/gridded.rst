============
Gridded Data
============

Marine heatwaves in global daily sea surface temperature (OSTIA, 1982-2022) on a
regular lat/lon grid. The first notebook detects extremes with two anomaly and
threshold combinations and compares them, the second tracks the events with
merging and splitting, and the third visualises and summarises them.
